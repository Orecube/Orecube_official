Research principles

How we approach the work.

  • Evidence before confidence

    Distinguish evidence, interpretation, and product hypothesis.

  • People before engagement

    Measure usefulness, learning, wellbeing, and real-world outcomes, not only time spent.

  • Access by design

    Consider language, disability, device, data, geography, and affordability from the beginning.

  • Human agency

    Technology should support understanding and decisions, not replace human judgment or relationships.

  • Responsible development

    Safety, privacy, transparency, and evaluation should be part of product development from the start.
