How we work

A practical, people-centered approach.

  • Useful

    Technology should help people make progress on real problems.

  • Thoughtful

    Capability should be developed with attention to limitations, context, and consequences.

  • Human-centered

    Products should be understandable, approachable, and designed around the people who use and are affected by them.

  • Responsible

    Safety and responsibility belong in research and product decisions, not only at the end.
